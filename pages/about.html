Our story

Every child gets a stage

We started SpeakUp Stage because confident speaking shouldn't depend on being born loud. It can be learned, joyfully.

Our mission

Turn quiet voices into confident ones

We help children aged 5–16 express their ideas clearly, kindly and bravely, in class, at home and on any stage life gives them.

Our method blends theatre games, structured speaking technique and lots of real stage time. Batches are small, coaches are trained to celebrate effort, and parents get a clear view of progress every week.

The SpeakUp method

Four steps, repeated every week

A simple loop that turns nervous first words into confident performances, at any age.

  1. 01

    Play

    Theatre games warm up voices and melt nerves before anyone is asked to speak.

  2. 02

    Practise

    One clear skill a week: pauses, gestures, story hooks or a strong opening line.

  3. 03

    Perform

    Every child takes a spotlight turn on our mini-stage or on camera, every class.

  4. 04

    Reflect

    Kind “glow and grow” feedback, plus a progress note for parents.
