Syllabus, week by week

  1. Week1

    Speech structure

    Hook, three points, close.

  2. Week2

    Openers that grab

    Questions, facts and stories.

  3. Week3

    Elocution technique

    Diction, modulation and pauses.

  4. Week4

    Extempore I

    60-second topics with the PREP method.

  5. Week5

    Research basics

    Facts vs opinions, citing sources.

  6. Week6

    Debate intro

    Motions, sides and rebuttals.

  7. Week7

    Body language

    Stance, movement and hand use.

  8. Week8

    Showcase #1

    5-minute speeches.

  9. Week9

    Handling nerves

    Breathing, anchors and recovery.

  10. Week10

    Extempore II

    Two-minute impromptu rounds.

  11. Week11

    Persuasion

    Ethos, pathos and logos for kids.

  12. Week12

    Mock elocution

    Judged round with scoresheets.

  13. Week13

    Feedback lab

    Self-review from video.

  14. Week14

    Debate practice

    Team rounds.

  15. Week15

    Rehearsal

    Polish competition pieces.

  16. Week16

    Final stage

    Public performance and awards.
