State Junior Elocution Championship

Our flagship elocution event, judged by theatre professionals and educators. Open to all students in Tamil Nadu.

Register now

Rules

  1. Pieces must be in English and suitable for a family audience.
  2. Speak from memory. One prompt card is allowed for Seniors only.
  3. A bell rings 30 seconds before time. Overrunning by 15+ seconds costs 2 points.
  4. Props and costumes are not allowed. Gestures are encouraged.
  5. Report 45 minutes before your category's slot. Judges' decisions are final.
