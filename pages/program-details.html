Syllabus, week by week

  1. Week1

    Hello, Stage!

    Name games, voice warm-ups and a 30-second ‘all about me’ talk.

  2. Week2

    Voice volume & pace

    The ‘dial’ game: whisper to stadium voice, slow to fast.

  3. Week3

    Story skeletons

    Beginning, middle, end with picture prompts.

  4. Week4

    Facial expressions

    Mirror pairs and emotion charades.

  5. Week5

    Poem recitation I

    Choosing a poem, marking pauses and stress.

  6. Week6

    Mini-showcase #1

    Recite to parents on the studio stage.

  7. Week7

    Gestures that help

    Open hands, pointing and ‘freeze’ moments.

  8. Week8

    Persuasive kids

    Why my favourite food is the best: 1-minute speech.

  9. Week9

    Listening & questions

    Asking and answering on stage.

  10. Week10

    Story with characters

    Different voices for each character.

  11. Week11

    Rehearsal week

    Peer ‘glow and grow’ feedback.

  12. Week12

    Grand showcase

    Final performance and certificate ceremony.
