Rhymes, show-and-tell and pretend play that turn shy whispers into happy, clear voices.

Syllabus, week by week

  1. Week1

    My name, my voice

    Circle games and ‘big voice, small voice’.

  2. Week2

    Show-and-tell I

    Bring a toy, say three things about it.

  3. Week3

    Animal voices

    Sounds and movements that build expression.

  4. Week4

    Rhyme time

    Nursery rhymes with actions.

  5. Week5

    Eye contact games

    Look-and-wave, spotlight torch game.

  6. Week6

    Mini-showcase

    Sing-and-say for families.

  7. Week7

    Feelings faces

    Happy, sad, surprised: show it!

  8. Week8

    Picture stories

    Telling a 3-picture story.

  9. Week9

    Show-and-tell II

    Answering one question from friends.

  10. Week10

    Puppet talk

    Speaking through a puppet to beat shyness.

  11. Week11

    Rehearsal

    Practising the final rhyme or story.

  12. Week12

    Little stage day

    Performance, stars and a certificate.
